DAY 3 - QUESTION 6: Categorical Prediction in WEKA (Trees, Preprocess, Logistic)
========================================================================
Tasks:
(a) Tree-based classification
(b) Preprocessing
(c) Logistic Regression

Step-by-Step WEKA Guide:
1. Preprocess:
   - Load dataset into WEKA Explorer.
   - Apply 'weka.filters.unsupervised.attribute.Discretize' for numeric attributes or 'NominalToBinary'.
2. Decision Tree (J48):
   - Under Classify tab -> Choose 'trees -> J48'.
   - Run 10-fold cross validation.
   - Right-click result item -> 'Visualize tree'.
3. Logistic Classifier:
   - Under Classify tab -> Choose 'functions -> Logistic'.
   - Review Odds Ratios, Confusion Matrix, and Accuracy metrics.
