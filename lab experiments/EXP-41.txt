DAY 4 - QUESTION 12: Standard Deviation & Variance Calculation
========================================================================
Dataset (9 Cars):

n = 9

1. Mean Calculations:
   - Mean(AvgSpeed)  = (78+81+82+74+83+82+77+80+70) / 9 = 707 / 9 ≈ 78.56 kph
   - Mean(TotalTime) = (39+37+36+42+35+36+40+38+46) / 9 = 349 / 9 ≈ 38.78 mins

2. (a) & (b) Variance and Standard Deviation for AvgSpeed:
   - Sum of squared differences from mean:
     (78-78.56)^2 + (81-78.56)^2 + (82-78.56)^2 + (74-78.56)^2 + (83-78.56)^2 + (82-78.56)^2 + (77-78.56)^2 + (80-78.56)^2 + (70-78.56)^2
     = 0.31 + 5.95 + 11.83 + 20.79 + 19.71 + 11.83 + 2.43 + 2.07 + 73.27 ≈ 148.22
   - Sample Variance (s^2) = 148.22 / (9 - 1) = 18.53
   - Sample Standard Deviation (s) = sqrt(18.53) ≈ 4.30 kph
   (Population Variance sigma^2 = 16.47, Population sigma = 4.06 kph)

     (39-38.78)^2 + (37-38.78)^2 + (36-38.78)^2 + (42-38.78)^2 + (35-38.78)^2 + (36-38.78)^2 + (40-38.78)^2 + (38-38.78)^2 + (46-38.78)^2
